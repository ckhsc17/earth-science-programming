MODULE STRUCTURE:
----------------
- mod_okada: Empirical point-source approximation
  * Fast computation (~10% of slip as max displacement)
  * Distance-dependent decay (near/mid/far field)
  * Suitable for far-field tsunami modeling
  
- mod_okada_dc3d: Complete DC3D finite fault
  * Integration over rectangular fault plane
  * Four-corner integration method
  * Accurate near-field and far-field
  * Uses original Okada (1985) formulation

================================================================================
THEORETICAL BACKGROUND
================================================================================

EMPIRICAL METHOD:
- Displacement ≈ 10% of slip (empirically determined)
- Distance decay using characteristic fault dimension
- Fast, numerically stable
- Good for tsunami far-field propagation

DC3D METHOD:
- Complete Okada (1985) analytical solution
- Surface deformation in elastic half-space
- Real-source + image-source contributions
- Four-corner integration for rectangular fault
- Accurate for all distances
